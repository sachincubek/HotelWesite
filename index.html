What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 10:00 AM and check-out is by 9:00 AM. 24-hour check-in may be possible by prior arrangement; please confirm with the host.

Can I enquire about parking or meals?

Please ask the host to confirm parking availability and current food options, including any pure-vegetarian arrangements.

How do I confirm a room and rate?

Send an enquiry with your dates and group size. Room availability, facilities and rates are confirmed directly by the host; this website does not take bookings or payments.
